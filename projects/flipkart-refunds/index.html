Flipkart Refunds Platform

Built and scaled the Flipkart refunds infrastructure — instant refunds via Visa Direct, MasterCard Send, and UPI, cutting SLA from 3-5 business days to 24 hours.

JavaDropwizardDistributed SystemsFintechPayments

Overview

Worked on the Flipkart refunds platform during the SDE phase — spanning infrastructure improvements, critical integrations, compliance, and reliability engineering.

Key Contributions

  • Instant Refunds — integrated Visa Direct, MasterCard Send, and UPI Refunds to reduce refund SLA from 3-5 business days to 24 hours.
  • Red-line Load Testing — conducted the first red-line load test for the refunds system, handling 10x BAU load. Identified bottlenecks and tuned the system for peak sale hours.
  • Incident Prevention — proactive participation during external partner outages prevented SLA breaches on 30-40k refunds at a time.
  • Infrastructure Migrations — managed VM to Kubernetes migration, DB version upgrades, and DC migration — resulting in 50% fewer on-call incidents.
  • RBI Tokenisation Compliance — contributor to the critical RBI tokenisation compliance program from the refunds team.
  • Azkaban Workflow Engine — set up Azkaban for automating and scheduling multiple refund processing jobs.

Tech

Java, Dropwizard, Kubernetes, distributed systems, payment network integrations (Visa, Mastercard, NPCI).
